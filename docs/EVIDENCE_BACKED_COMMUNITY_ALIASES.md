# Evidence-backed community hunt aliases

Second-pass aliases use both the community title and the observed monster/kill composition.

Examples:
- two Undead Dragon community labels → Grounds of Undeath (4/4 observed monsters, 100% kill coverage);
- Bashmu → Salt Caves (2/2, 100%);
- Cobra Basement → Cobra Bastion (3/3, 100%);
- Burster Spectre Darashia → Haunted Tomb (Darashia + complete observed compatibility);
- Prison -3 → Roshamuul Prison (6/6, 100%);
- Warzone 5 modifier variants → Warzone 5;
- Werecrocodiles → Murky Caverns (3/3, 100%).

The raw community title is always retained.

Still intentionally unresolved:
- Energy Library / Fire Library: distinct subareas that should not be collapsed into a broad Secret Library metric;
- generic or ambiguous labels such as Bosses, Crypt Warrior, Terrorsleeps, Pirats;
- aliases where identical monster sets occur in multiple canonical places and the title does not disambiguate them.
